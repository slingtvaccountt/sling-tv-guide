# Sling TV on Roku

Having trouble using Sling TV on Roku? This guide covers common Roku and Sling TV problems, including app issues, login problems, buffering, playback errors, and activation problems.

## Sling TV Not Working on Roku

If Sling TV is not working correctly on your Roku device:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart the Sling TV app.
3. Restart your Roku device.
4. Check for Sling TV app updates.
5. Check for Roku system updates.
6. Try opening Sling TV again.

## Sling TV App Not Opening on Roku

If the Sling TV app does not open:

- Restart your Roku device.
- Check your internet connection.
- Make sure the Sling TV app is updated.
- Check for Roku system updates.
- Remove and reinstall the Sling TV app if necessary.

## Sling TV Login Problem on Roku

If you cannot sign in to Sling TV on Roku:

1. Check your Sling TV email address.
2. Make sure your password is correct.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. Restart the Sling TV app.
5. Try signing in again.

If you forgot your password, use the password recovery option through the Sling TV sign-in process.

## Sling TV Buffering on Roku

If Sling TV keeps buffering on Roku:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart your router if necessary.
3. Restart your Roku device.
4. Close other applications or reduce network activity.
5. Try streaming again.

## Sling TV Freezing on Roku

If the video keeps freezing:

- Restart the Sling TV app.
- Restart your Roku device.
- Check your internet connection.
- Check for app updates.
- Check for Roku system updates.

## Sling TV Black Screen on Roku

If Sling TV displays a black screen:

1. Close and reopen the Sling TV app.
2. Restart your Roku device.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. Try another channel or program.
5. Check for available updates.

## Sling TV Playback Problems on Roku

If a video does not play correctly:

- Check your internet connection.
- Restart the Sling TV app.
- Restart your Roku device.
- Try another channel.
- Update the Sling TV app.

## Sling TV Activation on Roku

If Roku asks you to activate Sling TV:

1. Open the Sling TV app on Roku.
2. Follow the activation instructions shown on the screen.
3. Use the requested activation information.
4. Make sure you are using the correct Sling TV account.
5. Complete the activation process and return to the app.

## Sling TV Audio Problems on Roku

If Sling TV video plays but there is no sound:

1. Check the Roku volume.
2. Check your TV or speaker volume.
3. Make sure the TV is not muted.
4. Restart the Sling TV app.
5. Restart your Roku device.

## Update Sling TV on Roku

Keeping the Sling TV app updated can help resolve compatibility and performance problems.

Check your Roku device for available updates and install them when appropriate.

## Reinstall Sling TV on Roku

If the Sling TV app continues to have problems after restarting the app and device, reinstalling the app may help.

Before signing in again, make sure you know the email address and password associated with your Sling TV account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is Sling TV not working on Roku?

Check your internet connection, restart Roku and the Sling TV app, and make sure both the app and Roku system are updated.

### Why is Sling TV buffering on Roku?

Buffering can be caused by an unstable or slow internet connection. Check your network and restart your router if necessary.

### Why is Sling TV showing a black screen on Roku?

Restart the Sling TV app and Roku device, check your internet connection, and try another channel.

### Why can't I log in to Sling TV on Roku?

Check your email address and password and make sure your Roku has an active internet connection.

### How do I fix Sling TV freezing on Roku?

Restart the Sling TV app and Roku device, check your connection, and install available updates.
