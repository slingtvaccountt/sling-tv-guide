## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I log in to Sling TV?

Check that your email address and password are correct. You can also try refreshing the page or signing in from another browser or device.

### Why is Sling TV not working?

Check your internet connection, restart the Sling TV app and device, and make sure the app is updated.

### Why is Sling TV buffering?

Buffering can occur because of an unstable or slow internet connection. Check your connection and restart the app or device.

### Where can I get Sling TV login help?

You can use the Sling TV Login guide from the website navigation to find information about common login and account access problems.
